Pre-Release Score Estimator

Paste the IMDb link of a series that has not finished airing. Three Random Forest models estimate its audience score from what is known before release: genres, network, episode count, age rating, and the earlier work of its cast and creators.

Model benchmarks

Measured on series from the hold-out years, which were never used for training or tuning. The baseline always guesses the training average. MAE is the average miss in score points (lower is better).

balance

Read this honestly. The models beat the baseline by only a few percent. Audience ratings depend mostly on things nobody knows before release (writing, acting, marketing, reception), so pre-release data can only nudge the estimate. Treat every result as a rough guess, not a forecast.

Methodology

Data

Targets

IMDb and TMDB are the sites' own user ratings. General is a weighted average of the two, weighting each by log(1 + its vote count). Each target has its own model.

Inputs known before release

Genres, network, episode runtime and count, age rating, adaptation or remake flags, and the earlier IMDb work (average rating, best rating, number of titles) of the top-billed cast and the creative team. Only work from before the series' premiere year counts, so nothing from the future leaks in.

Validation

Limits

Trained on English-language, US-origin scripted series. Other languages, countries, or reality shows are outside its scope. For an unreleased series, cast and crew are often only partly announced; the Data completeness panel shows what was found, and missing inputs lower reliability.

Course project

Built for a classical machine-learning course. Models are scikit-learn pipelines, published on Hugging Face. Not financial or professional advice.

Runs entirely in your browser: the models are downloaded once, and the only lookups sent out are to TMDB and OMDb.
